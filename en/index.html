CatFinder

Find, report and keep track of the stray cats in your neighborhood.

CatFinder gathers the stray cats reported by the community onto a single map. Post a cat you found with photos, location and health status; check the ones already reported near you; and confirm a sighting when you see one again, so the people who rescue know where and when it was last seen.

How it works

  • Report

    Add up to three photos, describe the cat and mark its health status. Tags help others find it.

  • See the map

    The map shows the cats reported around you. Adjust the search radius to widen or narrow the area.

  • Follow the CatFeed

    The most recent reports in your area, in a list you can filter by radius and by tags.
